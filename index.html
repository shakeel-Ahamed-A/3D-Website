Before you arrive

Everything you need. Nothing hidden.

Who can participate?

Students, recent graduates, and independent builders may enter. Teams can include members from different institutions.

What should I bring?

Your laptop, charger, student or government ID, and any approved hardware required for your arena.

Is the event free?

Registration is free. Shortlisted teams receive full venue access for all three days.

Where is the venue?

City Convention Campus. Exact gate, travel guidance, and check-in instructions are included in the confirmation message.
